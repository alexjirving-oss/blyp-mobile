Why Blyp beats TikTok for creators & fans

TikTok is huge. Blyp is focused. We're building the place where live-first creators and serious fans get a better deal.

Big legacy platforms (like TikTok)

Coins & fees
  • Coins purchased mainly through app stores with high platform fees.
  • Large share of spend taken before it reaches the creator.
Live vs everything else
  • Live is one feature among many, competing with feed, ads and trends.
  • Tools and UX often optimised for overall watch time, not live depth.
Creator relationship
  • One of millions of creators in a massive, general audience app.
  • Limited visibility into how payouts and rankings are calculated.
Web experience
  • Most value flows through mobile app only.
  • Less emphasis on web-first monetisation for creators.

Blyp

Coins & fees
  • Web-first coin top ups via blyp.world to reduce store fees.
  • Designed so more of each coin can flow directly to creators.
Live-first design
  • Product built around live streaming and real-time interaction from day one.
  • Features designed for deep sessions: gifts, boosts, coins, and community tools.
Creator relationship
  • Focused on high-energy live creators and the people who actually show up for them.
  • Clear, transparent messaging about coins, balances and payouts as we roll out.
Web + app together
  • Landing page and coins experience designed to work alongside the mobile app.
  • Web is part of the core monetisation strategy, not an afterthought.

What this means in practice

  • Fans keep more impact when they support a creator on Blyp via web coins.
  • Creators get a platform tuned around live, not just generic content feed metrics.
  • We can iterate quickly with our community instead of optimising for a billion-user average.
