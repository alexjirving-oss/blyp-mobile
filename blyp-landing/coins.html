Blyp Coins

Blyp Coins are the currency that powers gifts, boosts and rewards across the Blyp ecosystem. They're designed so that more of what fans spend actually reaches creators.

Why buy coins on web?

When coins are purchased inside mobile apps, app stores can take a significant percentage of the transaction. Buying via blyp.world reduces that overhead, which allows us to:

  • Give fans better value bundles for the same money.
  • Pass a higher share of revenue directly to creators.
  • Run more flexible promotions and rewards.

How coins will work

We are finalising the design of the Blyp wallet and coin system. The current plan is:

  • Top up your Blyp balance securely via web.
  • Use coins across live streams and short-form content for gifts and boosts.
  • Transparent history of your top-ups and spend.

For creators

Our goal is simple: creators should keep more of what their audience spends to support them. The web-based coin system is being built with this in mind, so that the ecosystem stays sustainable.

Status: pre-launch

Coins via web are not live yet. We're working through payments, compliance and security so that when it goes live, it's stable and ready for scale.

If you're a creator or early user and want to be first in line for web-based coins, please reach out to the team.

Nothing on this page is financial advice. Details of bundles, pricing and creator payouts may change as we iterate.
