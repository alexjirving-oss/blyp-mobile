What makes Blyp different

Everything a live-first creator actually needs

Six things built into Blyp from day one — designed around live, community and a fair deal for the people who show up.

A "For You" feed that learns

Short videos ranked by who you follow, your interests and real engagement — with a touch of freshness so the feed never feels stale or repetitive.

Live streaming + co-hosting

Go live in seconds, bring guests on as co-hosts, and react in real time with your community. Live isn't an afterthought here — it's the centre of the app.

Creator-vs-creator Battles

Schedule a head-to-head live battle, invite an opponent and split the screen. Viewers vote and gift to crown a winner — with reminders and push so nobody misses it.

Coins & gems gifting

Top up Blyp Coins and gift them to the creators you love. Gifts turn into gems creators keep — a clean, closed-loop economy, never resold as cash.

Earn your reach

Every post gets a fair "audition" with the right people first. Genuinely liked posts go wider in waves. Boost buys a bigger test — never a place at the top.

"Blyp it" AI + reminders

Ask anything in plain language and get a concise answer blended with real posts and creators. Say "remind me to…" and Blyp schedules it for you.

The headline feature

Battles: two creators, one live arena

Pick an opponent, set a time, and go live together in a split-screen showdown. Your supporters get a reminder and a push notification so they show up — then vote and gift to decide the winner.

  • Schedule in a tap — in 15 min, tonight, or tomorrow at 8pm.
  • Optional equal coin deposit acts as an attendance bond, held in escrow.
  • Both show up → stakes returned. A no-show → the other takes the pot.
  • Glory is the real prize — winners are decided by the crowd, not the wallet.

Reach is earned

Good content climbs. Paying can't fake it.

Every new post is shown to a fair sample of the right people first. If they genuinely enjoy it, it earns the next wave — and the next. Reach is driven by a transparent score built on real signals, not by who paid the most.

  • A fresh "audition" guarantees every post is actually seen.
  • Merit moves you wider; resting posts are gently quieted, never hidden.
  • Boost buys a bigger, faster test — weak content still won't fly.
