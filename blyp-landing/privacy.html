Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 1, 2025

Welcome to Blyp. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, phone number, password, and profile information (including profile picture and bio)
  • Content: Videos, live streams, photos, comments, messages, and other content you create or share
  • Payment Information: Payment card details and billing information when you purchase coins or other in-app purchases (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
  • Communications: Information you provide when you contact us for support or feedback

1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you use Blyp, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Usage Information: How you interact with the app, including videos watched, search queries, likes, comments, shares, and viewing duration
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile network information, and device settings
  • Location Information: Approximate location based on IP address or precise location if you grant permission
  • Camera and Microphone: Access to your device camera and microphone when you create videos or live streams
  • Log Data: Server logs including IP address, access times, app crashes, and system activity

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third parties, including:

  • Social media platforms if you connect your account
  • Analytics and advertising partners
  • Payment processors

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve our services: Deliver content, personalize your experience, and develop new features
  • Recommendations: Provide personalized content recommendations and suggestions
  • Communication: Send you technical notices, updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • Safety and security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and other harmful activities
  • Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Analytics: Understand how users interact with our services to improve performance and user experience
  • Advertising: Show you relevant advertisements (you can opt out of personalized ads in settings)

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

3.1 Public Content

Your profile information, videos, live streams, comments, and likes are publicly visible to other users by default. You can adjust privacy settings in your account.

3.2 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, including:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers
  • Payment processors
  • Analytics services
  • Content delivery networks
  • Customer support tools

3.3 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

3.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests, including to:

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our rights and property
  • Prevent fraud or security issues
  • Protect the safety of users or the public

3.5 With Your Consent

We may share information with third parties when you give us explicit consent to do so.

4. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

4.1 Account Settings

You can update your account information and privacy settings at any time within the app.

4.2 Content Control

You can delete your videos, comments, and other content at any time. Some content may be retained in backups for a limited period.

4.3 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of promotional emails by following the unsubscribe instructions in those emails.

4.4 Location Information

You can disable location tracking through your device settings.

4.5 Your Legal Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information
  • Data portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to processing of your information
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@blyp.world.

4.6 Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Blyp account and associated data here: https://blyp.world/delete-account.

You can also request deletion of specific data without deleting your account by following the instructions on that page.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Specific retention periods include:

  • Account information: Retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period after deletion
  • Content: Retained until you delete it (may persist in backups for up to 90 days)
  • Analytics data: Typically retained for 90 days
  • Payment records: Retained for tax and accounting purposes as required by law

6. Children's Privacy

Blyp is not intended for users under the age of 13 (or applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us immediately at privacy@blyp.world, and we will delete the information.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws. We take appropriate measures to ensure your information receives adequate protection.

8. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

Our app may contain links to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@blyp.world
  • General inquiries: hello@blyp.world
  • Website: https://blyp.world

12. Additional Information for Specific Regions

12.1 California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

12.2 European Union Residents (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including those listed in Section 4.5 above. Our legal basis for processing includes:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide our services
  • Consent: Where you have given consent
  • Legitimate interests: For analytics, security, and service improvements
  • Legal obligation: To comply with applicable laws

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
