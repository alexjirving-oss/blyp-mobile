# The Blyp Charter

> This document is the foundation Blyp is built on. It is written to be read by anyone -
> not buried, not lawyered into silence. It states plainly what we stand for, how the
> platform works, and the promises we hold ourselves to. Where this Charter and our formal
> legal Terms differ in wording, the Terms are what is legally binding - but this is the
> honest gist, and we intend to live by it.

## Why Blyp exists

The big platforms behave in ways we think are wrong, and that is the reason for Blyp.

- **Google** makes money when search slightly fails - more ad clicks. It sells the top of the page through a blind auction, so businesses pay a tax to be found against their own name. It hides how it ranks anyone, promotes its own products, and uses creators' and publishers' work to answer on its own page while sending them less and less traffic.
- **TikTok** hides "the algorithm" entirely, pays creators poorly, lets posts be made only once, and allows coordinated reporting to punish people who have done nothing wrong.

We believe a fairer, transparent platform can win **because** it is fair. We are not trying to absolve ourselves of responsibility - we are trying to be honest about what we do, and to do right by the people who use us.

A note on ambition, made from the start: **we are not chasing $400 billion or strip-mining the economy.** We want a bit more than our fair share - never a ruthless one. Shopkeepers should pay a fair ad wage into the Blyp ecosystem and keep the profit they deserve. A sustainable, fair business that strengthens its community beats a giant that extracts from it. That is a deliberate choice.

## Our values

- **Transparency is the product.** Blyp shows its working. A brand-new platform paying little is fine if people can see exactly why.
- **Maximum safe transparency.** We disclose as much as we safely can - the principles, the signals we use, all of your own data, and the exact reason for any moderation action. We hold back only the minimum that would let people abuse the system, and we are transparent that we do so and why.
- **Aligned incentives.** Success is your task being done fast - even if that sends you away. We never profit from making search slightly worse.
- **Honest from zero.** No subsidies, no faked traction, no pay-to-rank. We grow honestly, even if it is slower.
- **A fair split.** Creators get the majority - their fair share - of the revenue their content earns. Blyp keeps a smaller, but real and sustainable, share. The exact numbers are set from measured costs, openly.
- **Contextual, not surveillance.** Ads are matched to what you searched, never to a hidden profile of you.
- **Your data is yours to trade, not ours to take.** We never sell you by default. Any data sharing is opt-in, explained in plain words at the moment it matters, and pays you back a share of what it earned.
- **Free to be found.** You never pay to appear for your own name. Verified businesses get a free, accurate listing; money only buys richer presentation, never rank.
- **The line is the law, not taste.** Illegal content means zero tolerance. Lawful-but-unpleasant content is not ours to referee - you curate your own experience.
- **Accountability earns power, not access.** Anyone can join with minimal friction. Influence over others (reporting weight, and privileges like payouts, live and advertising) is earned through verification and a good track record.
- **Bold, not timid.** We say what we mean in plain words, and protect ourselves with careful drafting rather than by saying nothing.

## How distribution works - "earn your reach"

This is the heart of Blyp, and the opposite of both TikTok's secrecy and Google's pay-to-win.

- **Every post auditions.** A new post is shown first to a seed audience - mostly people likely to care about it, plus a small random/crossover slice so genuinely great content can break out of its niche.
- **It climbs on merit, by rate not raw counts.** We measure *how well people react per view* - watch-through, saves, shares, follows, minus skips, hides and reports. React well, and it graduates to a wider audience, wave by wave. This gives new and niche creators a fair shot; a big account cannot coast on size.
- **Boost buys a bigger, faster audition - never a seat.** You can pay to enlarge the seed sample and speed up the waves, so you get a quicker, stronger read. But only merit converts that into reach. A boosted post that people do not enjoy still goes nowhere - and we can prove it.
- **Posts can be edited.** Unlike TikTok, you can improve a post. Small tweaks keep its score; a substantial change (swapping the video, a major rewrite) relaunches it as a new version with a fresh audition - which also stops anyone baiting reach with good content and swapping in something else.
- **You get a clear Blyp Score** for every post, broken down into its parts, and you can see exactly why it did or did not climb.

## Transparency - the dashboard, for everyone

Every user gets a transparency hub (from your profile, a small home entry, and a "Why am I seeing this?" shortcut on any post). It has two halves:

- **What Blyp is doing** - the same for everyone: the real rules of distribution, ranking, ads and payouts in plain language, plus live aggregate stats. No hidden rules.
- **What you are doing** - private to you: the data we hold, why you saw a given post, and - for creators - each post's score, audition history and earnings; for advertisers, what a boost actually bought and the honest organic result it earned.

**Your account rating** lives here too - a standing only you see by default, that you can be proud of. Behave well and it stays high. A single complaint shows a visible dip ("we noticed - benefit of the doubt") and bounces back if it was nothing; a pattern makes it slide. It governs your trust and safety standing only - **never your content's reach**, which stays purely merit-based.

When we feed back on a post, the tone is coaching, not punishment ("most viewers swiped away in the first two seconds - try a stronger hook").

## Trust, safety and accountability

We want the coordinated-reporting abuse that plagues other apps to be impossible here.

- **Two lanes.** Illegal content is removed, the account banned, evidence preserved, and the authorities informed where the law requires it - zero tolerance, no debate, and for child-safety material, no second chance, ever. Everything lawful-but-nasty is not adjudicated by us: you block, mute, filter and move on, and content that broad, independent audiences reject simply loses reach.
- **Brigading does not work.** Reports and blocks are weighted by the reporter's trust and track record, checked for coordination (a sudden swarm of connected or throwaway accounts is discounted, and the *coordinators* get flagged - not their target), and judged by proportion of real viewers, not raw numbers. Volume alone never removes anyone, and a live broadcast cannot be killed mid-stream by outsiders.
- **Reporting is accountable.** You are never anonymous to Blyp when you report - but you are anonymous to the person you reported, so victims can flag harassment safely. Abusing the reporting system costs you your standing.
- **Identity.** Signing up is easy and light. You can be pseudonymous to other users, but you are accountable to Blyp. Verification and a clean record unlock trust, and privileges like payouts, going live and advertising.
- **Reputation, shown the right way.** Your private *account rating* (how you're doing on behaviour) stays private to you, with the forgiving dip-then-recover shape — we never put a public score over your head to be gamed or weaponised. What *is* public is a small, **positive-only standing badge** on your avatar wherever it appears (on posts, live, comments): tiers like Verified → Trusted → Established/Legacy, earned through verification, time and a clean record. It can only ever say something good; there is no public "low score", because a public number invites pile-ons and status anxiety. Good standing is visible and aspirational; bad standing is handled privately and fairly.
- **Fair process.** Moderation decisions come with the exact reason and an appeal, and the penalty ladder for non-illegal behaviour leans forgiving - a warning, then reduced reach, then a temporary suspension, then a ban - with your standing recovering through good behaviour. Repeat or severe behaviour escalates firmly.
- **Where you are matters.** Rules are applied with your country in mind, and lawful adult content is age-gated rather than removed.

We cooperate generously with authorities on genuine harm, but we word our commitments carefully and we do not over-share beyond what is lawful and necessary. (Our formal Terms, reviewed by lawyers, govern the binding detail.)

## Money

- **Creators first.** The majority of the revenue a creator's content earns goes to the creator. When your content is used to answer a search, you share in what that search earned. You can see the maths.
- **Ads that respect you.** One clearly labelled sponsored slot, capped in density, matched to your query (not to a profile of you), and it can never outrank the top honest result. Pricing is simple and published - never a blind auction.
- **Fair to business.** Free verified listings so you are never charged to be found for your own name; you only pay for genuine extra reach via Boost (which still has to earn it) or richer presentation.

### Plans — try everything, then choose

- **30-day full trial.** Everyone starts with the whole app unlocked for 30 days. You can cancel any time before it ends and pay nothing. Subscriptions are billed through the app store (Google Play / Apple) — so the trial, cancellation and renewal are handled by the platform you trust, and we never touch your raw card details for it.
- **Free tier (after trial).** Nothing is taken away that you made or rely on socially — your posts, follows, messages, gifting and feed all keep working. What pauses are the *premium AI conveniences* (AI captions/hashtags/titles, Blyp AI answers + voice, AI-enhanced sport pages). The app stays genuinely useful for free; the magic AI layer is the paid upgrade.
- **Blyp Plus — $4.99/mo.** Everything on, all the AI features, forever-fresh.
- **Blyp Plus + Coins — $9.99/mo.** Everything in Plus, **plus 999 coins every month**. The headline: the coins are worth roughly what you paid, so the subscription effectively pays for itself in spendable currency. Coins can be gifted to creators or converted to gems — they are **never** cash-withdrawable, and the $9.99 is not redeemable for money. (This keeps the coin a clean, closed-loop in-app currency, not stored value.)

The honest framing we always show: a paid plan buys *features and currency*, never reach or a place at the top of the feed. Reach is still earned.

## Data, only as a fair exchange

We do not harvest and sell you by default. If an advertiser or retailer wants access to data, we ask **you** directly, in plain words, at the moment it matters - never a buried tick-box at the end of a form. The deal is explicit and mutual:

- A share of what the buyer paid comes **back to you**.
- You get better, more relevant deals because of the better understanding.
- You can always say no, with zero penalty, and change your mind later.

Most data sharing online is *taken*. Ours is *given* - knowingly, transparently, for something real in return. We believe far more people say yes to that than to a checkbox, precisely because it's honest.

## Our opening moves

How Blyp introduces itself matters. The first thing you experience should be us giving, not charging.

- **Free advertising to start.** Every business gets a genuine free advertising gift when they join - real reach, no card required. We still charge fairly afterwards; we just never rip you off. This is the anti-Google handshake: we lead with generosity.
- **Bring your account with you.** Switching platforms shouldn't mean starting from zero - that's the trap that keeps people stuck. If you already have a following elsewhere (e.g. TikTok), Blyp can import your existing content and rebuild your account to an equivalent Blyp state automatically. Your work comes with you. Moving to the fairer platform should be effortless. **But the bar to claim someone else's account is real, not a formality:** to import and claim a handle you must (1) prove control of the source account — at least one reasonable piece of evidence, e.g. a one-time code we give you placed in the account's bio or a posted clip; (2) submit government ID; and (3) hold a verified phone number on Blyp. The import flow itself stays friendly and encouraging — we do **not** front-load threats. Fraud handling lives quietly in the binding Terms: attempts to claim an account that isn't yours are flagged for review and, when confirmed, reported — without tipping off the bad actor mid-attempt.
