Blyp Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Blyp is committed to protecting children and to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on our platform. We maintain a zero-tolerance approach to any content or behaviour that sexualises, endangers, or exploits children. This document describes our child safety standards in line with Google Play's requirements for social and communication apps.

1. Zero tolerance for CSAE / CSAM

We strictly prohibit, and act against, any content, conduct, or use of Blyp that involves:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind;
  • Sexualisation of minors, grooming, sextortion, or solicitation of minors;
  • Trafficking, endangerment, or any exploitation of children.

Violations result in immediate content removal, account termination, preservation of evidence, and reporting to the appropriate authorities.

2. Compliance with child safety laws

We comply with applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions in which we operate. Where we become aware of apparent CSAM, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or relevant local authorities, and cooperate with law enforcement as required by law.

3. In-app reporting and user controls

Blyp provides accessible mechanisms for users to report content, profiles, comments, and live streams that may violate these standards. Reports concerning child safety are prioritised for review. Users can also block other users and manage who can interact with their content.

4. Content moderation

We use a combination of user reporting, moderation review, and policy enforcement to detect and remove violating content. Confirmed violations lead to removal and, where appropriate, permanent account bans and escalation to authorities.

5. Minimum age

Blyp is not intended for children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required by your country). Accounts found to belong to underage users may be removed.

6. Child safety point of contact

To report a child safety concern, suspected CSAE/CSAM, or to reach our child safety team, contact us at safety@blyp.world. We review such reports as a matter of priority.

7. Designated reporting channels

In addition to in-app reporting and our contact address above, members of the public may report suspected child exploitation to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org or to their local law enforcement authority.
