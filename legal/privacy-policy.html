Blyp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blyp ("Blyp", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Blyp mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

Blyp is a social application for short videos and live streaming, where users can create and watch content, follow creators, and send virtual gifts purchased with in-app "Blypcoins". For privacy questions, contact us at safety@blyp.world.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and personalise the Service and your feed.
  • To process in-app purchases and maintain virtual currency balances.
  • To keep the Service safe: detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and policy violations.
  • To communicate with you about your account, security, and updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

5. Data security

Purchases are verified server-side and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are managed by our identity provider and stored in hashed form. While no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, we use reasonable safeguards to protect your data.

6. Data retention

We retain account and content data while your account is active. You can delete your content or request account deletion at any time (see Section 8). Certain records (e.g., purchase/transaction logs) may be retained where required for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact safety@blyp.world and we will delete it.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access & deletion: Request a copy or deletion of your data, or delete your account in-app, or by emailing safety@blyp.world.
  • Permissions: You can control camera, microphone, photo, and location access in your device settings.
  • Regional rights: Depending on where you live (e.g., UK/EEA under UK GDPR/GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have additional rights to access, correct, port, or restrict processing of your data.

9. International transfers

We operate globally and may process and store data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by additional notice in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: safety@blyp.world.
